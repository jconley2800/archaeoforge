# User visual brief

Create a photorealistic wide view of the Giza plateau newly completed in 2500 BCE.
Three great pyramids are sheathed in smooth polished white limestone casing with
sharp unbroken edges, gleaming almost mirror-flat in the sun. Small subsidiary
pyramids stand alongside. Long straight covered causeways run down to valley
temples at the desert edge. The sphinx is freshly carved, its features crisp and
its enclosure clear of sand. Clean desert, no rubble, no ruins. Warm low sunlight.

## Evidence-boundary note

This is the requested presentation brief, not archaeological evidence. The
simultaneous newly completed state is a chronological composite. In particular,
the requested wholly white and perfectly finished Menkaure casing conflicts with
the documented granite lower casing and incomplete surface finish. Those conflicts
must remain disclosed in the evidence register and final provenance.

## Historical-correction addendum

After reviewing the waterfront evidence, the user requested a further pass that
prioritizes verified history, adds detail where archaeology supports it, and uses
only sober likely completion where the record is unclear. For the next version,
this instruction supersedes the earlier all-white, simultaneously-new visual
alternative wherever it conflicts with stronger evidence. The target remains an
interpretive view at approximately 2500 BCE, with uncertainty made visible rather
